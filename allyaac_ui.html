📚 IMU Telemetry Physics, Axes & Sway Measurement Guide

Comprehensive clinical reference for 9-DoF linear acceleration, inclinometry, postural sway computation, and shockwave filtering.

50 Hz Movesense IMU
Ax — Surge Axis
Longitudinal (X)

Measures forward and backward motion along the length of the forearm.

Physical Actions: Reaching arm forward, pulling back, or tilting the forearm upward/downward.
Inclinometer Role: Directly drives Pitch angle calculation relative to Earth's gravity: atan2(Ax, √(Ay²+Az²)).
Ay — Sway Axis
Lateral (Y)

Measures side-to-side / lateral motion across the wrist or wheelchair tray.

Physical Actions: Waving hand left-to-right, lateral tremors, or pronation/supination twisting.
Inclinometer Role: Drives Roll angle calculation relative to Earth's gravity: atan2(Ay, Az).
Az — Heave Axis
Vertical (Z)

Measures vertical up-and-down motion perpendicular to the sensor pod face.

Physical Actions: Lifting the arm upward, tapping or knocking down against a table or wheelchair tray.
Gravity Vector: When resting flat palm-down, Az registers ~9.8 m/s² (1.0G) of Earth gravity.
🔬 Can We Calculate Sway? (Clinical Posturography & Biomechanics) RMS • Path Length • Peak-to-Peak

Yes, absolutely! In clinical biomechanics and vestibular rehabilitation, Sway represents the involuntary or voluntary displacement of the body or limb away from equilibrium. Using the Movesense 50Hz telemetry stream, we calculate Sway in three ways:

1️⃣ Instantaneous Lateral Sway

Raw lateral acceleration on the Ay axis isolated from static tilt. A sudden spike in Ay indicates an intentional lateral swipe or wave gesture.

Sway_lat(t) = Ay(t) - g · sin(Roll)
2️⃣ RMS Postural Sway (Root Mean Square)

Standard clinical metric for tremor or ambient wheelchair vibration. Measures standard deviation of sway across a sliding time window.

RMS_Sway = √( 1/N · Σ (Ay_i - μ_y)² )
3️⃣ 2D Sway Path Length (COP / Trajectory)

Cumulative distance traveled by the wrist in the horizontal plane (Ax vs Ay). Used in neurology to evaluate ataxia and motor control stability.

Path_Length = Σ √( (ΔAx)² + (ΔAy)² )
Total Acceleration Magnitude (|M|) Scalar Sum

The total instantaneous Euclidean norm of acceleration across all 3 spatial axes:

|M| = √( Ax² + Ay² + Az² )

At complete rest in any rotational orientation, |M| = 9.8 m/s² (1.0G) because Earth's gravitational pull is constant.

Dynamic Surge Shock (|M| - 9.8) Orange Trace

Subtracting static Earth gravity isolates pure kinetic shockwaves and voluntary gesture energy:

Surge = |M| - 9.8 m/s²

Resting reads 0.0 m/s². A table knock or arm flick creates an immediate positive spike used by the Tier 0 Kinematic engine for trigger detection.
